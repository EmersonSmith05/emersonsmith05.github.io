How it works

The monthly routine

  1. Download the statement PDF from americanexpress.com and drag it in (or paste the charges).
  2. Fix any orange rows — pick the right category and choose "Remember" so it's automatic next month.
  3. Check for ✓ Reconciled in the stats — that means every charge was captured and matches the statement total to the penny.
  4. Download the Excel, or copy the totals for the accounting system.

Where your data lives
Everything — statements, rules, jobs — is stored inside this browser on this computer. Nothing is uploaded anywhere; there's no account and no server. That also means: use Back up everything (Export panel) once in a while, and keep the backup file in the Statement Sorter folder so OneDrive protects it. Restore a backup brings it all back on any computer.

What you can drop in
Amex statement PDFs, charges pasted from the Amex website or a Word doc, Amex CSV downloads, and the bills Expense List spreadsheet. If a file won't read (scanned image, unusual bank), open it, copy the charge text, and use the paste box instead — the parser just needs dates and amounts.

Little extras
The + on any row assigns a job, splits a charge across categories/jobs, or adds a note. Insights is the charts page, Library holds every saved statement, and the Export panel prints branded monthly and 12-month reports.
