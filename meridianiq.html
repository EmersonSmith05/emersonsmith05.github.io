MeridianIQ is a customizable portfolio intelligence platform for RIAs, wealth managers, family offices, and investment teams. We don't force your investment process to fit our software — we adapt the software to fit your investment process.

Launch Advisor demo Launch Admin demo Explore the marketing page

Firm-level models, not client accounts

MeridianIQ manages your firm's model portfolios. Client accounts, tax lots, and books of record stay where they belong — in your custodial systems.

Honest numbers

We never fabricate, seed, or estimate financial values. Missing data renders as missing, every value carries provenance, and estimates are labeled as estimates.

Built for firms

Roles, audit trails, versioned allocation history, and a propose → preview → approve → publish workflow that keeps official records official.

Features

  1. Managed rebalance history — propose, preview side-effect-free, approve, publish. Effective-dated versions; corrections supersede with actor and reason.
  2. Performance & risk — trailing returns, drawdowns, Sharpe/Sortino, benchmark capture ratios, with explicit methodology and caveats.
  3. Screener — score any portfolio, watchlist, or ticker set against your firm's own thresholds, weights, and directions.
  4. Stress testing — editable historical scenarios with coverage-honest estimates.
  5. Validate-then-apply imports — holdings, allocation history, prices, and metrics; validation writes nothing and reports exactly what would happen.
  6. Firm administration — users, roles, benchmarks, modules, and a separate platform-owner surface.

How firms work with us

  1. Evaluate in an isolated demo — provenance-labeled demonstration data, no live connections.
  2. Choose the lawful data path — file imports today; licensed provider connectors by written agreement.
  3. Adapt the platform to your workflow — base analytics, or the custom strategy tier with proprietary models.
